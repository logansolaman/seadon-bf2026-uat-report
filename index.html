BUG (confirmed live): legacy per-product discount shadows the BF campaign on same-variant quantities

When 2+ units of the same variant of EXP / Trail / Shoreline / Cropped / Verano / Daymaker Tank are on one line, an old legacy price rule (per-unit "$X off", plans labelled legacy / product-legacy in the quote payload) overrides the Black Friday campaign. Different colours of the same product price correctly. These totals reproduce the tester's doc exactly (9/9 rows).

Root cause: a legacy quantity discount (roughly 10% off at qty 2, 30% off at qty 3, per product) still exists on those product groups in Shopify. It wins priority over the BF campaign for same-variant carts, producing (a) wrong bundle totals and (b) the nonsensical result that EXP ×3 ($129) costs less than EXP ×2 ($134). Fix = delete/deactivate the legacy per-product discounts for EXP, Helios Trail, Shoreline, Cropped, Verano and Daymaker Tank so the BF campaign plan serves all quantities.

Verdict

28 of 40 scenarios priced correctly. 12 failed — every failure is real money off the table or overcharged at checkout. Two root causes were identified:

  • Root cause 1 — BF Single prices ignored. The deployed checkout proxy prices from MSRP. Affected: Gale ACT (always $100 over), Tech Tank single ($34 vs $20), Hugger Beanie single ($40 vs $25), and every "leftover unit" beyond a bundle tier (Sherpa, EXP, Shorts).
  • Root cause 2 — Tier spread instead of tier fill. Ladder quantities spread the discount proportionally across all lines instead of filling the best tier first. Affected: Tech Tees x5 ($140 charged vs $110 correct).

This is why the totals look wrong at checkout — the math disagrees with the published bundle table in exactly these cases.

The rule in one sentence

The moment a single line contains 2+ units of the SAME variant, an old per-product "legacy" discount (about 10% off qty 2, about 30% off qty 3) overrides the Black Friday bundle pricing — the quote payload literally switches from "plan": "campaign" to "plan": "legacy", "source": "product-legacy". Mixed colors and separate lines price perfectly. This both undercharges (customer pays less than the published bundle price) and creates the absurd 3-for-$129 < 2-for-$134 inversion. Fix: delete the legacy per-product discounts in Shopify Admin on EXP, Trail, Shoreline, Cropped, Verano and Daymaker Tank.
